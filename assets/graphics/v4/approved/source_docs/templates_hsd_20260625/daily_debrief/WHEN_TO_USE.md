# THE DAILY DEBRIEF — Final Cleanup When-to-Use Notes

## Variant A — Carousel system
Use when the Daily Debrief deserves a full carousel experience: cover slide, story slide, and end-question slide. Best for days with three strong cross-sport stories.

## Variant B — Single-image summary card
Use when HSD needs one fast Feed or Threads graphic that summarizes the day’s top three stories in one card.

## Variant C — Story / Vertical Roundup
Use when HSD needs a quick vertical Daily Debrief for Stories or Reels with three stacked story blocks and space for an optional poll/question sticker.
