## League rules

### 1. WNBA

- Priority: must_cover_daily
- Frequency: daily
- Rule: At least one WNBA touch every day, even on non-WNBA-led days.
- Best formats: Tonight in the W, Last Night in the W, Daily Debrief, milestone post, Commissioner’s Cup stakes
- Angle bank: Who needs this win more?, Commissioner’s Cup pressure, Rookie watch, Star watch, Statement win, Upset watch, Injury impact only if verified

### 2. WTA / women’s tennis

- Priority: must_cover_when_story_strong
- Frequency: 4 to 6 times per week if strong
- Rule: IG feed only when there is a big name, comeback, upset, or clear Wimbledon implication.
- Best formats: big-name watch only if verified, grass-court watch, Wimbledon lead-in, upset post, American tennis watch
- Angle bank: Serena comeback/appearance watch only if officially verified, Wimbledon loading, American women on grass, Upset watch, Teen/rising star watch, Comeback stories, Grass-court chaos

### 3. NWSL / women’s soccer

- Priority: high
- Frequency: 3 to 5 times per week
- Rule: Second identity after WNBA; use soccer to make HSD feel bigger than basketball.
- Best formats: Weekend preview, club growth post, rivalry/storyline post, business/culture post, USWNT follow-up
- Angle bank: NWSL weekend watch, Club building like a giant, USWNT aftermath, Attendance/culture growth, Transfer watch, Player spotlight, Who is actually the best team?

### 4. LPGA / women’s golf

- Priority: medium_high
- Frequency: 2 to 4 total posts
- Rule: Do not overpost golf yet; use it to make HSD feel smart and broad.
- Best formats: smart culture/business post, major lead-in, player spotlight, why this matters explainer
- Angle bank: Women’s golf has a team event, LPGA stars to know, KPMG major loading, Why women’s golf deserves more attention, Nelly/Lydia/Lilia watch if relevant

### 5. Women’s volleyball / VNL

- Priority: medium
- Frequency: 1 to 2 posts per week
- Rule: Post only when the context can be made simple for casual followers.
- Best formats: global women’s sports post, teams to know, USA volleyball watch, quick explainer
- Angle bank: Women’s volleyball is having a moment, USA volleyball watch, VNL teams to know, Global women’s sports board, Olympic stars are back

### 6. College softball evergreen

- Priority: low
- Frequency: 1 max
- Rule: Use as evergreen only; main event has passed.
- Best formats: legacy recap, championship hangover, Texas dynasty angle
- Angle bank: Texas dynasty check, Teagan Kavan legacy post, Why college softball keeps growing

### 7. Women’s college basketball offseason

- Priority: low_unless_news_breaks
- Frequency: only when warranted
- Rule: Only use for major transfer, commitment, injury, coaching move, schedule announcement, or culture moment.
- Best formats: recruiting, transfer, injury, coaching, schedule, viral athlete moment
- Angle bank: Recruiting watch, Transfer watch, Coaching move, Schedule drop, Viral athlete moment

## Formula

- WNBA every day

- LPGA and volleyball as smart secondary pillars
