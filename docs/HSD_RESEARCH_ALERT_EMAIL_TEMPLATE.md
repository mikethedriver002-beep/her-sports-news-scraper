# HSD Research Alert Email Template

Status: operator alert template.

Research alerts should be emailed to Mike when an external ChatGPT Pro or Gemini Pro pass would materially improve a decision, but active Codex lanes do not need to stop.

Default recipient: `michael@brieffactory.com`

## When To Email

Send a research alert email when:

- A renderer decision needs visual taste, sports design research, or reference comparison.
- A large contact sheet is ready for human/external visual review.
- A source strategy spans many leagues, teams, or athlete identities.
- A legal/fair-use/right-class question needs broader analysis.
- A roadmap decision affects multiple implementation lanes.

Do not email for routine PR status, passing tests, or minor lane chatter.

## Subject Format

`HSD research alert: <tool> - <lane> - <short task>`

Examples:

- `HSD research alert: Gemini Pro - renderer - compare latest draft to references`
- `HSD research alert: ChatGPT Pro - soccer assets - source strategy audit`

## Body Template

```text
Mike,

Research alert:

Tool to use:
<ChatGPT Pro or Gemini Pro>

Why now:
<one sentence explaining the decision this improves>

Packet to upload:
<absolute local zip/folder path>

Prompt to paste:
<exact prompt>

Expected output:
<ranked findings, risks, and 5 PR-sized packets>

Codex continues:
<yes/no; if yes, say which lane continues while you run this>

Guardrails:
- review-only
- no paid APIs by default
- no auto-approval
- no publishing
- no publish-ready lane
- no asset-state changes without human-edited intake
- downloads only through approved quarantine intake
```

## Sending Rule

The conductor may send these emails directly when a real research packet exists and the action is time-sensitive or likely to improve the next implementation packet.

If the alert is exploratory or low urgency, the conductor should create a Gmail draft instead of sending.
