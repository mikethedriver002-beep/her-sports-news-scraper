# HSD Graphics Law v1 Appendix: LPGA / Golf

This appendix is part of HSD Graphics Law v1. It locks the LPGA / Golf template family into the governed HSD template system.

## LPGA / Golf template law

The LPGA / Golf family has three review-approved directions:

### Variant A: Winner / Champion Card

- Use for an LPGA winner, champion moment, major victory, trophy-focused result, or major final-round win.
- Must include editable fields for PLAYER NAME, TOURNAMENT NAME, WINNER / CHAMPION, RESULT / SCORE, WHY IT MATTERS, and QUESTION / CTA.
- Trophy and golf visuals are abstract editorial design elements unless approved real event imagery is provided.
- Approved player image slots and approved event logo slots are optional.

### Variant B: Leaderboard / Standings Update Card

- Use for leaderboard updates, top five standings, final-round positioning, projected cut context, tournament status, or leaderboard-based storylines.
- Must keep the leaderboard readable and not table-heavy.
- Round/status field must be editable.
- Allowed round/status examples include ROUND 1, ROUND 2, ROUND 3, FINAL ROUND, AFTER 18, AFTER 36, and PROJECTED CUT.
- Leaderboard rows should support position, player name, score, and movement/note fields.

### Variant C: Story / Vertical Golf Update

- Use for quick LPGA or women’s golf updates on Stories or Reels.
- Export at 1080x1920.
- Allowed modes: winner/champion update, leaderboard snapshot, final-round story, or player spotlight.
- Only one mode should be active at a time.
- Bottom area must stay clear for Story/Reels UI.
- Next-update language must be editable. Allowed fields include NEXT UPDATE, ROUND STATUS, FINAL ROUND NOTE, and WATCH THIS SPACE.
- Do not bake platform poll UI into the graphic. Use POLL / QUESTION STICKER SPACE or QUESTION / CTA as the editable area.

## Shared LPGA / Golf rules

- Keep golf-specific course, trophy, leaderboard, and final-round energy while still feeling like HSD.
- Approved player images only.
- Approved tournament/event logo slots are optional unless an approved logo is provided.
- Abstract golf and trophy textures are allowed as editorial design backgrounds.
- Abstract textures must not imply real event photography or replace approved imagery.
- Use clean editable placeholder fields such as PLAYER NAME, TOURNAMENT NAME, RESULT / SCORE, LEADERBOARD POSITION, ROUND STATUS, NEXT UPDATE, FINAL ROUND NOTE, WATCH THIS SPACE, WHY IT MATTERS, and QUESTION / CTA.
- Do not invent scores, results, positions, rankings, records, quotes, injuries, or tournament details.
- Public mockups must not show internal variant/template/spec labels.
- Use placeholder copy unless real details are provided.
